Critter Ranch Tycoon

Catch · Sell · Grow your ranch

Stand on the balcony and throw capsules to catch cute critters, stack them on your back, sell them to customers on the conveyor belt, and spend your coins on pens, helpers and upgrades. A casual arcade-idle game for Android.
