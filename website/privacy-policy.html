Privacy Policy — Critter Ranch Tycoon

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains how Sandipan Sarkar ("we") handles information in the mobile game Critter Ranch Tycoon (the "App").

Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Game progress is saved only on your device.

Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Google's use of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Rewarded ads

Some in-game bonuses (extra coins, boosts) can be earned by voluntarily watching a rewarded ad. Watching ads is always optional.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the App deletes all game data stored on your device. You can also use Settings → Reset progress.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
